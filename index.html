What We Do

Water Damage Restoration Services — Peoria, IL

Burst pipe at 2am in January. Basement flood from spring snowmelt. Sewage backup through the floor drain. Every situation is different — here is what the contractors we connect you with handle.

Water Extraction

Truck-mounted extraction units remove standing water, water trapped in carpet and pad, and surface water from hard floors. Far more powerful than any consumer equipment. Contractors follow with thermal imaging to find hidden moisture in wall cavities before drying begins.

Structural Drying

Industrial dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers placed per IICRC S500 psychrometric calculations. Daily moisture readings documented at mapped points. Drying continues until materials reach target moisture content — incomplete drying causes mold.

Basement Flooding Cleanup

Peoria basements flood from spring Illinois River snowmelt runoff, heavy rain overwhelming sump pumps, and groundwater seepage in river-basin neighborhoods like Chillicothe. Contractors handle standing water removal, mud cleanup, and structural assessment.

Frozen & Burst Pipe Response

Peoria winters regularly drop below zero. When temperatures swing rapidly — below zero to 40°F in 24 hours — pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces burst. Local contractors respond same-day to extract water and begin drying before damage spreads.

Sewage & Contaminated Water

Category 3 black water (sewage, outside floodwater) cannot be dried — contaminated materials must be removed per IICRC S500. Contractors handle containment, removal, EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment, and HEPA air scrubbing.

Mold Remediation

Water damage that wasn't dried properly — or sat hidden in a wall — leads to mold. Contractors assess, contain, and remediate per IICRC S520. Independent clearance testing available through a certified industrial hygienist.

Commercial Water Damage

Office buildings, medical facilities, and commercial properties in the Peoria and East Peoria area. Contractors staff commercial jobs appropriately to minimize downtime and provide the documentation insurance adjusters require.

Insurance Claim Assistance

The contractors we connect you with work with all major Illinois carriers — State Farm, Country Financial, Pekin Insurance, USAA, Allstate, Farmers. Complete moisture logs, photos, and Xactimate-compatible scope of work. Contractors communicate directly with your adjuster.

Why Speed Matters

Water Damage Gets Worse Every Hour

This is not a scare tactic — it is the documented timeline from the IICRC S500 standard. The faster water is extracted and drying begins, the lower the total damage and the lower the final claim.

1h

First Hour: Spread & Absorption

Water migrates under flooring, into wall cavities, and through drywall. Carpet pad absorbs and holds water. The longer it sits, the more surface area is affected — and the more material must be replaced rather than dried.

24h

24 Hours: Drywall Damage Begins

Drywall swells, warps, and loses structural integrity. Hardwood flooring starts to cup. Subfloor materials absorb water. In Peoria's older homes with finished basements, 24 hours can turn a $3,000 job into a $10,000+ rebuild.

48h

48 Hours: Mold Growth Can Begin

The IICRC S520 standard identifies 24–48 hours as the window in which mold can begin growing on wet organic materials at normal room temperatures. Once mold is present, remediation scope and cost increase significantly.

7d

One Week: Structural Compromise

Framing lumber, subfloor OSB, and structural components begin to deteriorate. In Peoria's pre-1980 housing stock with older framing, prolonged saturation can compromise structural integrity faster than in newer construction.
