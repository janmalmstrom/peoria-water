Water Damage Restoration Services

IICRC certified extraction, drying, mold remediation, and insurance documentation — Peoria, IL & surrounding areas

First Response

Water Extraction

Extraction is the first and most critical step. The goal is to remove as much water as possible before structural drying begins.

We use truck-mounted extraction units. After extraction, thermal imaging cameras locate hidden moisture before drying equipment is placed.

IICRC S500 Standard

Structural Drying

Industrial dehumidifiers placed based on psychrometric calculations. Daily moisture readings logged for insurance documentation.

Midwest Winter Hazard

Frozen & Burst Pipe Response

Peoria winters regularly drop below zero. Same-day response to burst pipe emergencies regardless of weather conditions.

Category 3 — Black Water Protocol

Sewage Cleanup & Contaminated Water Remediation

Sewage backup and floodwater are Category 3 (black water). Contaminated porous materials must be removed per IICRC S500.

IICRC S520 Standard

Mold Remediation

Mold can begin growing within 24–48 hours. Containment, removal, HEPA vacuuming, antimicrobial treatment, and air scrubbing.
